# Interview synthesis

Six freelancers who signed up in August. We asked about the last invoice they sent, not about our product. The questions follow [[The Mom Test]].

## Patterns
- **Bank details are the wall.** Four of six stopped at connecting a bank account. Two did not have their details at hand and meant to come back later. Only one did.
- **They want to see the invoice first.** Every person asked what the invoice would look like before setting anything up.
- **They work alone.** Five of six have no one to invite. The sixth shares the account with a bookkeeper.

## Quotes
- "I send maybe three invoices a month. I just want the first one out."
- "I stopped when it asked for my IBAN. My banking app was on the other phone."
- "Show me the PDF. That is what my client sees."

## What changes
- Draft the invoice before the bank account.
- Drop "Invite your team" from the first-run checklist.
- Ask for the bank account on the send screen, where it clearly matters.
