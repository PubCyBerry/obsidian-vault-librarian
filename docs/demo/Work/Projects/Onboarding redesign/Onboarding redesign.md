# Onboarding redesign

## Goal
New accounts send their first invoice within ten minutes. Today only 38% of new accounts send one in their first week.

## What we know
- People stall at connecting a bank account, not at writing the invoice. See [[Interview synthesis]].
- Most drop-offs happen on the second screen of the setup wizard.

## Plan
1. Replace the setup wizard with a checklist that stays in the sidebar.
2. Let people draft an invoice before they connect a bank account.
3. Track each step so we can see where people stop.
