# Competitor notes

Looked at five invoicing tools for freelancers in September.

- Everyone offers a free tier. Most cap it by the number of clients, not invoices.
- Three of five hide the yearly price behind a toggle. Two show it by default with "save 20%".
- Only one explains what happens when you hit the free limit. That page felt the most honest.
- None of them mention the bank connection on the pricing page, although it is the first thing setup asks for.

## Ideas to borrow
- A single sentence under each plan that says who it is for.
- A short FAQ right under the cards, not on a separate page.
