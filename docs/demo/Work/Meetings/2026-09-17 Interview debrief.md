# Interview debrief

Six interviews with freelancers who signed up in August. Notes per person are in [[Interview synthesis]].

## What we heard
- Four of six stopped at connecting a bank account.
- Five of six work alone. Nobody asked to invite a teammate.
- Everyone wanted to see the invoice before setting anything up.

## Decisions
- Drafting an invoice comes before the bank account.
- "Invite your team" moves out of the first-run checklist and into settings.
