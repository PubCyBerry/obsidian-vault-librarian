# The Mom Test

People lie to be nice when you ask about your idea. So ask questions that even your mom could not lie to you about.

## Takeaways
- Talk about their life, not your idea.
- Ask about specific things they did in the past, not what they might do in the future.
- Talk less and listen more.
- Compliments are noise. Commitments, such as time, money or an introduction, are signal.

## Where I used it
- The onboarding interviews. "Tell me about the last invoice you sent" worked far better than "Would you use a checklist?" See [[Interview synthesis]].
